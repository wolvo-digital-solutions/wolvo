# WOLVO Website — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product

WOLVO official company website.

## 2. Product vision

Create a premium 3D interactive website that communicates WOLVO as a digital technology + creative studio and converts qualified visitors into project enquiries.

The site should combine:
- strong brand storytelling
- real trust signals
- premium 3D visual identity
- full-page scroll choreography
- service clarity
- portfolio credibility
- founder visibility
- clear calls to action

## 3. Primary goals

### Business goals
- Explain WOLVO's services clearly.
- Establish credibility.
- Showcase real work.
- Introduce the founder and co-founder.
- Communicate the studio's global ambition without making unsupported claims.
- Generate project enquiries.
- Create a memorable brand experience.

### UX goals
A visitor should understand:
1. Who WOLVO is.
2. What WOLVO does.
3. What WOLVO has built.
4. Who is behind the company.
5. How WOLVO works.
6. How to contact WOLVO.

## 4. Audience

Primary:
- startup founders
- small and medium businesses
- creators and personal brands
- organizations needing apps/websites
- businesses needing creative/marketing support
- companies seeking an external digital partner

## 5. Core services

### App Development
Custom mobile applications and digital product experiences.

### Web Development
Modern websites and web applications.

### Video Editing
Commercial/social/brand video editing.

### Graphic Designing
Brand identity, social creatives, marketing graphics, digital design.

### Advertising Management
Paid campaign setup, management, optimization and reporting.

### Social Media Management
Content planning, creative coordination, publishing and social presence management.

Use final copy only after WOLVO team approval.

## 6. Social proof

Current known metric:
- 10+ happy clients

Only use verifiable metrics on the website.

## 8. Hero requirements

Visual:
- 3D WOLVO wolf
- deep midnight environment
- WOLVO blue/cyan lighting

Animation:
Wolf activates → energy → geometric decomposition → W formation → logo transition.

Copy:
`TURNING IDEAS INTO DIGITAL IMPACT.`

Supporting message:
`WOLVO is a digital creative and technology studio building apps, websites, brands, campaigns and digital experiences for ambitious businesses.`

Primary CTA:
`START A PROJECT`

Secondary CTA:
`EXPLORE OUR WORK`

Do not place too much text over the 3D scene.

## 9. Trust section

Show:
- 10+ Happy Clients

Optional future metrics must be added only after verification.

## 10. Services UX

Each service should have:
- service title
- concise description
- relevant 3D visual or motion
- CTA
- optional technology tags

Services should share a consistent visual system.

## 11. Portfolio

Each case study should support:
- project name
- client
- service(s)
- challenge
- solution
- technology
- outcome/results
- image/video
- external link where available

Do not invent project results.

## 12. Process

Steps:
1. Discover
2. Plan
3. Design
4. Build
5. Launch

Animate the journey using a progressive timeline.

## 13. About

Communicate:
- why WOLVO exists
- what the team believes
- mission
- vision
- working philosophy

Avoid unsupported history.

## 14. Founders

Two profiles:
- Founder
- Co-Founder

Each:
- real photo
- name
- role
- short biography
- expertise
- social links where available

Do not generate fake people.

## 15. Technology

Show only technologies actually used by the company.

Potential examples to be verified with WOLVO:
- React
- React Native
- JavaScript
- TypeScript
- Python
- Django
- PostgreSQL
- Firebase
- Supabase
- Git
- GitHub

## 16. Testimonials

Use real client testimonials only.

Each testimonial:
- quote
- name
- company
- role if approved
- optional project reference

## 17. Global vision

Visual:
- cinematic 3D globe
- blue connection points
- subtle network lines

Content:
Position WOLVO as aiming to work with clients globally.

Do not claim existing international operations without evidence.

## 18. Final CTA

Headline:
`HAVE AN IDEA? LET'S BUILD IT.`

CTA:
`START A PROJECT`

Support with short enquiry-oriented text.

## 19. Conversion requirements

Primary conversion:
- contact/enquiry

Secondary conversions:
- view work
- explore services
- social links

Contact form fields:
- name
- company
- email
- phone (optional)
- project type
- budget range (optional)
- message

## 20. Success criteria

The website is successful when:
- content is immediately understandable
- 3D motion enhances rather than obscures information
- all sections work on mobile
- page loads progressively
- no major runtime errors remain
- real portfolio and trust content is visible
- visitors have a clear path to contact WOLVO
